## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a cinematic, high-end macabre aesthetic tailored for curated horror props, lifelike bespoke mannequins, and nocturnal concierge delivery. The experience rejects seasonal kitsch and cartoon tropes in favor of an occult, gallery-grade atmosphere—blending minimalist negative space with high-tension theatrical luxury.

The emotional core evokes quiet dread, exquisite craftsmanship, and dark romance. Visual compositions mimic a candlelit high-fashion showroom: generous expanses of ink-black abyss, razor-sharp editorial typography, and disciplined, deliberate crimson accents that command immediate focus. Interfaces feel tactile, brooding, and precisely engineered.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict darkness, utilizing deeply saturated undertones to create depth without relying on generic gray scales:

- **Abyss Background (`#0B0014`)**: The foundational void. Absorbs light, heightens contrast, and grounds all screens.
- **Surface Elevation Layers**:
  - `surface-dim` (`#11041F`): Secondary canvas backing and nested containers.
  - `surface-container` (`#160726`): Base card backgrounds, elevated sheets, and drawer panels.
  - `surface-elevated` (`#220B3B`): Active interaction states, hover planes, and floating overlays.
- **Royal Occult Purple (`#6A0DAD`)**: Primary brand identity, featured iconography, focus highlights, and verified authentic insignia.
- **Haunting Crimson (`#C80000`)**: High-stakes action accents, express checkout CTAs, rush delivery badges, and error states. Used sparingly to preserve urgency.
- **Phantom Bone White (`#F2F2F2`)**: High-contrast, pristine neutral for display titles, crisp structural iconography, and primary body content.
- **Ghost Mauve (`#A79BB7`)**: Muted secondary text, subtle perimeter borders, placeholder fills, and divider lines.

## Typography

The typographical pairing balances dramatic editorial grandeur with classical bookish legibility.

- **Playfair Display** commands headlines, product monikers, and narrative interludes. Its exaggerated contrast, sharp terminals, and sweeping serifs reflect antique grimoires and haute couture publishing.
- **Literata** grounds all body copy, item specifications, and UI elements. As an exquisitely proportioned literary serif, it provides crystalline legibility even at micro sizes on deep-tinted backgrounds while avoiding the sterile neutrality of sans-serif fonts.

All uppercase labels utilize wide tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to mirror luxury artifact identification plaques.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an expansive 4-column fluid mobile grid scaling to an 8-column layout for larger viewports, anchored by a 4px/8px structural cadence.

- **Mobile Canvas**: Screen edges maintain a default margin of `1.25rem` (`20px`) with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters between multi-column cards.
- **Vertical Air**: Rather than dense data grids, the system embraces intentional negative space (`space-xl` and above) between curated showcases, allowing individual mannequins and artifacts to stand isolated like museum exhibits.
- **Edge Restraint**: Safe areas for mobile notches and bottom home-indicators inherit full `#0B0014` bleeds to eliminate harsh interface seams.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through dark atmospheric stratification, subtle luminous edges, and selective vapor glows rather than standard black dropshadows:

1. **Layer 0 (Base)**: `#0B0014` flat void.
2. **Layer 1 (Card/Container)**: `#160726` surface bordered by a crisp `1px` stroke of `#A79BB7` at 15% opacity.
3. **Layer 2 (Floating Modals & Sheets)**: `#220B3B` backdrop blur surfaces (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` at 85% opacity) accompanied by an ambient purple aura (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(106, 13, 173, 0.25)`).
4. **Layer 3 (Urgent / Active Elements)**: Blood Crimson elements cast a short, intense back-light glow (`0 0 16px rgba(200, 0, 0, 0.40)`), creating the perception of a backlit ember in an unlit room.

## Shapes

The design uses a restrained, sharp-soft geometry (`level 1`). Base containers, product tiles, and text inputs utilize `0.25rem` (`4px`) radii, providing a tailored, chiseled silhouette reminiscent of obsidian tablets and architectural cabinetry.

Interactive pills, tags, and floating action toggles carry a full soft pill curve strictly when signaling ephemeral state, contrasting the otherwise rigid, dignified geometry of the catalog layout.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Rush Dispatch / Purchase)**: Haunting Crimson (`#C80000`) fill, bone white (`#F2F2F2`) label in `label-lg`, `0.25rem` corner radius. Subtle crimson drop illumination on active press.
- **Secondary (Customizer / View Relic)**: `#160726` background, `1px` border of `#6A0DAD`, phantom bone typography.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent base with Ghost Mauve (`#A79BB7`) text and an underline that tightens on interaction.

### Mannequin Customizer Sliders & Steppers
- Low-profile horizontal tracks filled with `#220B3B`, traversed by bone white circular handles with a faint crimson center pip. Value readouts display in tracked `label-md`.

### Cards & Specimen Displays
- Built upon `#160726` with a `1px` hairline border of `#A79BB7` at 12% opacity.
- Images occupy seamless edge-to-edge frame tops with a bottom gradient fade melting directly into the card body.

### Chips & Attribute Selectors
- Compact pills with `0.25rem` rounded corners.
- Inactive: `#11041F` background with `#A79BB7` text.
- Active: Royal Occult Purple (`#6A0DAD`) border with a soft inner wash of purple at 20% opacity and `#F2F2F2` text.

### Text Inputs & Search
- Recessed `#0B0014` field set within `#160726` containers. Ghost Mauve placeholder text. Focusing shifts the border from `#A79BB7` (20% opacity) to an ethereal `#6A0DAD` glow.

### Delivery Countdown & Dispatch Indicators
- Real-time night delivery radar badges styled as crisp crimson indicators with micro ping animations to underscore urgency and rapid fulfillment.
